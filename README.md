# MHDverse 🌍

**Learn. Connect. Grow.**

MHDverse is an educational community initiative designed to connect students with reliable educational information, university guidance, learning resources, and knowledgeable people.

Our goal is to make educational opportunities easier to understand and help students make informed decisions about their academic and professional futures.

## Vision

To build an informed, connected, and educated society where every student has access to useful educational knowledge and appropriate guidance.

## Mission

- Provide accessible educational information and learning resources.
- Help school students understand university pathways, faculties, degree programmes, and career opportunities.
- Connect students with undergraduates, teachers, lecturers, and other knowledgeable individuals.
- Build a supportive community that encourages learning, collaboration, and personal growth.
- Expand the initiative gradually from the Rajarata University of Sri Lanka community to other universities across Sri Lanka.

## Problem Statement

Students often struggle to find clear, reliable, and understandable information about educational pathways, university admissions, degree programmes, and future careers.

MHDverse aims to address this challenge through organized educational resources and a structured guidance process that connects questions with suitable people.

## Proposed Solution

MHDverse will combine educational content, community participation, and a structured question-and-guidance process.

When a student asks a question, the relevant volunteer or team will attempt to provide an accurate answer. If further expertise is required, the question can be escalated to an appropriate undergraduate, teacher, lecturer, or other knowledgeable person.

Information will be checked before it is presented as verified guidance.

## Target Audience

- School students
- Ordinary Level (O/L) and Advanced Level (A/L) students
- University applicants
- Undergraduates
- Students exploring academic and career opportunities

## Planned Features

- Educational articles, videos, and learning resources
- University and faculty information
- Degree programme and admission guidance
- Student question submission and guidance
- Volunteer and knowledge-contributor coordination
- Career and higher-education resources
- A digital platform to organize educational information and community interaction

*These are proposed capabilities; they should not be considered implemented until development is complete.*

## Development Roadmap

### Phase 5 — Expansion
- Connect additional universities and educational communities.
- Expand the volunteer network and educational resources.
- Introduce further features based on demonstrated needs.

## Accuracy and Trust

MHDverse aims to provide useful and reliable educational information. Admission requirements, programme details, deadlines, and other time-sensitive information should be checked against appropriate authoritative sources.

Where information has not been verified, its status should be made clear.
